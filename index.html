07 / FAQ

BEFORE WE
GET STARTED.

How long does a project take? +

Timing depends on the service, project size and how quickly feedback is provided. Your expected delivery date is agreed before work begins.

Are revisions included? +

Yes. The revision scope is agreed before the project starts so both sides are clear on what is included.

What files will I receive? +

Final formats depend on the service. Graphic projects can include JPG, PNG or PDF; presentations can include editable slide files and PDF; websites are delivered according to the agreed setup.

Can I request something not listed? +

Yes. Choose the closest service in the form and explain what you need in the project details.
